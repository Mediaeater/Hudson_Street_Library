Research log: kern_richard_girlfriend_boyfriend
Date: 2026-10-18
Shelf: Araki / Kern. Owner description: "pink and blue pair, Girlfriend and Boyfriend, two volumes". Originally briefed as Araki; corrected by the lead to Richard Kern.

IDENTIFICATION
- Title: Girlfriend Boyfriend (cover reads SHIZEN / GIRL FRIEND BOY FRIEND / RICHARD KERN in a pixel font). Kern's CV, the Jousse Entreprise CV, Wikipedia and Peter Miles Studio all give "Girlfriend Boyfriend". Shizen's own pages and Purple (Sept 2014) say "Boyfriend Girlfriend"; Bakunen says "Boyfriend, Girlfriend". Catalogued under the cover/CV order.
- Publisher: Shizen Books, the imprint of Salon Shizen (New York, opened 2005) and The Oversea (Omotesando, Tokyo, 2008). Kern's CV lists "The Oversea Books, New York" for this and for 10:41 (2011). Bakunen lists "Shizen Books".
- Year 2014; Purple (dated 16 Sep 2014) says forthcoming in October.
- Specs from Bakunen: 42 pages, 20.2 x 27.9 cm, softcover, first edition limited to 300. Peter Miles Studio archive: "Shizen, Richard Kern. Book. Girlfriend Boyfriend. Paper with Eska cover. 8 x 11 in."
- Credits from Shizen page: Photographer Richard Kern; Designer Peter Miles Studio; Hair YOKO, KOSHI, AKI; Styling Stephanie Boone; models Henry Stambler, Cody Chandle, Heaven Starr, Zac Scheinbaum, Nicole Van Staatum, Richard Wanger, Wade Oates, Katherine Clary, Jordan Robin, Natasha Nunez, Petra Collins, Gaby Bush, Bryce Hackford, May Hong, Jesse Hudnutt, Jane Moseley, Polina Skoch, Kaelin Ballinger, Abdul Kircher, Coley Brown.
- Shizen's preview PDF (5 spreads): boy on left page, girl on right, coloured seamless backdrops; staples visible in the gutter, so stapled.
- No ISBN found anywhere (AbeBooks keyword search: no matches; not on richardkern.com/books-1 list; no Dashwood/Innen/APE/Friends Editions listing found).

STRUCTURE DECISION
- One JSON (slug kern_richard_girlfriend_boyfriend). No source describes separate "Girlfriend" and "Boyfriend" titles; every source treats it as one 42-page publication with a single gradient cover (pink at top, blue at bottom), which matches "pink and blue". Whether the edition is physically two booklets or the library holds two copies is unverified; notes say "Two volumes." per the lead's instruction and flag this.
