IDENTIFICATION
Brief: spine read as "Contact Sheets" in script lettering; title uncertain.
Searched for a Kern title or zine built on contact sheets (web search, Innen, Dashwood,
Catalogue Library, Kern's own book list). No Kern publication titled "Contact Sheets"
or built on contact sheets exists in any source checked. Kern's complete book list on
richardkern.com/books-1 (30 items) has one title beginning "Contact": CONTACT HIGH (2013).
Its cover and spine title is set in italic script lettering ("Contact High" over
"Richard Kern" in serif), which matches the "script lettering" detail of the spine read.
"Contact" is an exact match; "Sheets" is taken to be a misread of "High" in script.
Other Kern titles considered and rejected: Grey Cards (2025, Friends Editions, grey card
pictures, title in caps), Polaroids (APE 2023), Polarized (2016), Gray (2023), Super High
(2024, title in red caps), Extra High (Innen, already id 569). None has a script title or
the word "Contact".
Confidence: medium-high. The only way to be sure is to look at the shelf copy: Contact
High is a tall 14 x 11 inch softcover with a bathroom photograph of a long-haired woman
exhaling smoke on the cover.
