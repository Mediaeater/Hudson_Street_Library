Item: Hiroshi Sugimoto, Nature of Light (杉本博司 光の自然), Izu Photo Museum, 2009

Spine read "Nature of Light" matches the published title. Cover and spine read
"HIROSHI SUGIMOTO  NATURE OF LIGHT"; NDL records the title as
杉本博司--光の自然 : Hiroshi Sugimoto Nature of light. Recorded as title "Nature of Light",
Japanese title in notes.

Conflicts and decisions
- Pages: NDL 151 (library record, authoritative) = Mullen 151; satelliteee 154; Fraenkel
  and Specific Object 112. Recorded 151.
- Dimensions: Specific Object 29.8 x 23.5 cm; satelliteee 29.8 x 23.8 x 2.2 cm; Fraenkel
  8.25 x 11.75 in (= 21 x 29.8). Recorded 29.8 x 23.5 cm.
- Publisher location: NDL says Izu Photo Museum (Nagaizumi); Sugimoto's site says Mishima
  (Clematis no Oka straddles the two). Recorded Nagaizumi, Shizuoka.
- Print run: not stated anywhere.
