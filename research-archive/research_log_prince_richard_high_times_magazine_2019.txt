Corrections to the brief:
- Not the "Trippy" issue. The Trippy issue was September 2016 (No. 488). This copy's cover says "Special Collector's Edition", August 2019, No. 523. Subtitle set accordingly; Printed Matter's "Trippy" label recorded in notes.
- No source says Prince guest-edited the issue; the cover credits him with the cover art and the "Katz and Dogg Special Edition Launch". Described as such.
- The 2018 Gagosian venue was West 21st Street, not West 24th.
- Editor credit (Dan Skye) comes from Wikipedia, not the masthead.
No price recorded.
