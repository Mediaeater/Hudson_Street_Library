IDENTIFICATION
Spine read: Taschen book with the "T-Bone" tattoo photo on the spine, 25th-anniversary edition.
Confirmed as Terryworld, ed. Dian Hanson, Taschen 25th Anniversary edition (2008).
- The 2008 edition (ISBN 9783836501910 / 3836501910) is marketed by sellers and in the shrinkwrap stickers as "TASCHEN celebrates 25 years! Special edition" and "Revised edition, includes over 70 tasteful new photos" (AbeBooks listing 32458660243, seller photo of a sealed copy).
- Seller photo https://pictures.abebooks.com/inventory/32500910934_3.jpg shows the jacket spine: Richardson's lower belly with the T-BONE tattoo and the gold "25 TASCHEN" anniversary logo at the head of the spine. Front = Richardson reclining shirtless (SSA heart, cross, eagle/flag tattoos), black TERRYWORLD lettering; back = his legs, hand over groin, Gemma Pearson / Fused Magazine quote, ISBN 978-3-8365-0191-0 printed (listing 32532096919 photos 2-4).
- Caveat: the spine photo came from a copy listed under the later 2012 ISBN 9783836535519 (which has a reversible PANIC! jacket band); the 25-logo jacket design appears to be shared across the 2008 and 2012 printings. The 2008 edition is the one sold as the 25th Anniversary special edition, so the edition field follows the brief.
- No other Taschen Richardson title carries that spine; the 2004 first edition (9783822822555) has gold-foil title lettering and no 25 logo.
