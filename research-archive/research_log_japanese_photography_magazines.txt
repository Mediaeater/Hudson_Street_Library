- Authors: the cover and publisher credit three editors (Kaneko Ryuichi, Toda Masako, Ivan Vartanian), so Toda is authors[1] and Vartanian authors[2], departing from the brief's two-editor assumption. Family-name-first on the cover; Western order in `name`. Macron dropped from Ryuichi in last/first to match row 2249 and the filename rule.
- Year: 2022 (Goliga listed the book Oct 2022, Aperture cites 2022). Photomonitor and Delpire say 2023, which looks like the Western distribution date. Check the colophon on the shelf copy; recorded as unresolved.
- Classification "Photobook", grouping "Photography", tags reused from row 2249 and the existing tag list (Photography, Japanese Photography, Magazines, Photography History, Reference, Japan).
- num_images 1500 per the publisher (Printed Matter/Delpire say 1,300).
