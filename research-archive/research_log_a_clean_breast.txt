IDENTIFICATION
Spine read "A Clean Breast!", blue cloth, "three copies". Search on the exact title
resolves to one book: Russ Meyer's self-published three-volume autobiography,
A Clean Breast! The Life and Loves of Russ Meyer (The Rural Fellini, His Films,
Fantasies und Fräuleins), as told by Russ Meyer to Adolph Albion Schwartz,
Hauck Publishing Company, Hollywood, 2000. Confirmed against the object itself in
dealer photographs (AbeBooks inventory 31902202244, 31760717638; AbeBooks ISBN image
9780962179709-us.jpg): light-blue cloth boards, Meyer's signature in gilt, "A clean
BREAST!" in gilt with the exclamation mark, and "VOL. 1" / "VOL. 2" / "VOL. 3" under
the title. The same-title Theresa Tan memoir (Write Editions, 2013) is unrelated.

THREE COPIES vs THREE VOLUMES
The book was issued as a set of three volumes in identical blue cloth. The three
blue-cloth books on the shelf are almost certainly the complete set (Vol. 1-3), not
three copies of one book. notes carries the required "Three copies in the collection,
blue cloth." line verbatim, followed by this caveat. Lead should check the volume
numbers on the front boards before ingest and amend notes if it is the set.

AUTHOR ENTRY
