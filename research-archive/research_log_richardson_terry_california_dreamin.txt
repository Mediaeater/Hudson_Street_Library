Research log: Terry Richardson's California Dreamin' (slug richardson_terry_california_dreamin)
Date: 2026-10-18. Shelf intake, Mollino / Richardson shelf. No date fields set (lead passes --intake catalogued).

Identification
- The spine title matches a Playboy one-off special edition: "Terry Richardson Special / Playboy / Terry Richardson's California Dreamin'", February 2015. Not Vanity Fair, not a zine. Publisher: Playboy (Amazon lists "PBI"; BookLikes lists Playboy Enterprises Inc.).
- Playboy statement of July 2014 (quoted by Fashionista, Fashion Gone Rogue, HuffPost): 100-page special edition titled California Dreamin', four Playmates, locations from Joshua Tree to the Chateau Marmont, newsstand around Valentine's Day 2015; 6-page preview in the July/August 2014 issue, further previews planned for December 2014 and January/February 2015.
- Jezebel 2015-02-25: on sale that week. Richardson posted it on terrysdiary.com (posts 112049569772 and 112049602737; domain now redirects, not fetched).
- Wikipedia "Playboy Special Edition": a one-off special edition published February 2015 with models in California locations by Richardson (no title given there).
- Cover (Hypebeast and Life Without Andy product shots): front cover headline "TERRY RICHARDSON SPECIAL", logo, "TERRY RICHARDSON'S CALIFORNIA DREAMIN'"; barcode block reads issue 05, display until May 2015. Back cover: model in cropped California top against sky, same title lettering.
