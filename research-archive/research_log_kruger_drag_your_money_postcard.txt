- Printed Matter publisher page for Fotofolio (catalog/publisher/718): Kruger cards stocked alongside this one include Memory Is Your Image of Perfection and You Invest In The Divinity Of The Masterpiece; also a Cindy Sherman and a Makos/Warhol card.

Source artwork
- Pinault Collection (lesoeuvres.pinaultcollection.com): Untitled (You can't drag your money into the grave with you), 1988, photograph and type on paper, 18 x 23 cm. First shown by the collection in Mapping the Studio, Palazzo Grassi / Punta della Dogana, 6 Jun 2009 to 10 Apr 2011; later in Untitled, 2020 (Punta della Dogana) and Forever Sixties (Couvent des Jacobins, 2023). Image description: new pair of leather shoes on grass, three white lines carrying the words.
- Skarstedt Gallery, Pre-digital 1980-1992 (18 Mar to 18 Apr 2009): same work listed, 1988, photograph and type on paper, 7 x 9 1/8 in (17.8 x 23.2 cm). Same checklist includes To Buy or Not to Buy (1987), Bleed Us Dry (1987), Money Can't Buy Me (1983).
- Sprueth Magers exhibition archive (Monika Sprueth Galerie, Cologne, 23 Mar to 26 Apr 1990): a 1990 screen print on vinyl of the same image, 276.9 x 377.5 cm. artnet records the same 1990 vinyl version.

Publisher
- fotofolioshop.com/about: Fotofolio / Artpost began in 1975 publishing the history of photography in postcard format; fine art and photographic postcards, notecards, posters; co-founder Juliette Galant; works with artist estates (Avedon, Mapplethorpe, Goldin, Haring, Basquiat, Tillmans, among others) and museum collections. Printed Matter's supplier record names Julie Gallant at Fotofolio.

Artist
- Wikipedia / barbarakruger.org: Kruger b. 1945, Newark; Pictures Generation; found black-and-white photographs with declarative text in white-on-red Futura Bold Oblique since the late 1970s. barbarakruger.com is a fan site, not official. barbarakruger.org is the official Catalogue Raisonne project and is used as the artist link.
