About the Series

One Picture Book Two is a continuation of Nazraeli Press's celebrated One Picture Book series. Each hardcover volume measures approximately 6×8.5 inches and presents a focused body of work by a single photographer. The distinguishing feature of the series is the inclusion of a removable, signed, original print measuring approximately 5×7 inches.

With editions strictly limited to 500 numbered copies, these books have become highly collectible. The series features an impressive roster of photographers including Michael Kenna, Daido Moriyama, Todd Hido, Alec Soth, Carrie Mae Weems, Eikoh Hosoe, John Divola, and many other acclaimed artists working in contemporary photography.
