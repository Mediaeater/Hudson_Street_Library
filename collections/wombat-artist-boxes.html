Wombat Artist Boxes

Limited edition photography portfolios produced by Wombat Paris. Each box contains museum-quality numbered prints on Hahnemühle paper, printed by Processus lab, and unpublished portfolios from contemporary photographers. Hand-assembled, numbered, and stamp-dated in limited editions.
