A

The mark system

one component · three sizes · dark green

The mark is the spines device ruled into a square and stamped in the library's dark green — #034706 on press, matched to the green the wordmark already carries at the head of every page on the site. It is drawn once and used at three fixed sizes — nothing else, and no piece goes without it. Below 10 mm the wordmark drops away and the seal carries the identity alone.

Rules
  • Square, ruled at 5.8% of its own width, so the frame scales with the mark.
  • Always #034706 — the same green on press and on screen. There is no print-only variant. Never a third colour. The black variant exists only for the 1-ink fallback.
  • Never smaller than 7 mm — the leaning spine closes up below that.
  • Clear space on all sides equal to one spine width.
  • The seal never sits inside a rule, box, or panel other than its own.
B

The family — Foolscap Mk II

six pieces · one mark · black + green

Six pieces, all carrying the same seal at its specified size. The head sheet is a pad — glued at the head, 50 sheets on a chipboard back — and it is not a plain letterhead: it is the accession record, with a two-column docket and a condition block using the antiquarian trade grades. What the design takes from Japanese stationery is restraint and a stamp: the boxed seal, the stacked wordmark, a 34 mm hairline in place of the full-width rule, confined ruling instead of ruling that bleeds across the page, and the form reference set vertically in the left margin.

One bookmark, not five. The collection is ticked by hand rather than printed, so there is a single SKU, a single plate set, and no ink swap — and a new collection never obsoletes the stock. It also puts the bookmark in the same idiom as everything else in the family: a form you fill in.

Printer's notes — bookmark
  • One SKU, one static forme. No versioning, no plate change, no wash-up between collections. Single run, single quantity.
  • 4/4 process-free: 2 spot colours both sides — black + PMS green — same two units, work-and-turn. Identical artwork on every sheet, so no imposition variants.
  • Imposition: 5-up on A4 (210 × 297), 10-up on SRA3 (320 × 450). Guillotine only — no die, no kiss-cut.
  • No bleed. All artwork inset 6 mm from trim; 3 mm trim allowance is sufficient.
  • Grain long, parallel to the 210 mm dimension, so the piece stays flat in a book.
  • Reprints need no new artwork: new collections are written into the Other rule by hand.

Notes to the printer — whole job
  • Colours: 2 spot, no process. Black, plus one green — the artwork is built at #034706, which is also the value the mark carries on screen. Match to a Pantone chip drawn down on the running stock rather than to the on-screen value, and allow for dry-back on uncoated when choosing the chip — but do not re-specify the artwork.
  • No bleed anywhere. All artwork inset a minimum 6 mm from trim on every item. 3 mm trim allowance is sufficient throughout.
  • No coatings, no varnish, no lamination, no foil, no emboss. Uncoated must stay writable — these are forms that get filled in with pen and pencil.
  • No die-cutting except the kiss-cut labels (item 05). Everything else is straight guillotine.
  • Grain long on all items, parallel to the longer dimension. Critical on the pad, or the sheets cockle at the head.
  • Pad build (01): 50 sheets, flexible padding compound at the head, 1.5 mm chipboard back, no tape strip. Jog to the head before gluing.
  • Overprinting stock envelopes (03): keep 5 mm clear of gummed areas and of the flap fold; reverse prints after the face, with make-ready for flap thickness.
  • Registration: the green only ever meets black at rules and the seal frame — ±0.15 mm is adequate. No trapping required.
  • 1-ink fallback: if the job is quoted black-only, the seal prints black and nothing else changes. Do not substitute a screen or tint for the green.
  • Proofs: one wet or press proof on the running stock for the pad and the bookmark; laser is adequate for the rest.
  • Delivery: labels on sheets, not rolls. Bookmarks banded in 50s. Pads shrink-wrapped in 5s.
