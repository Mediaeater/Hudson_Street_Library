Every piece is drawn in millimetres and shown at actual size, on screen and on paper: calibrate the screen against a bank card using the controls below, or switch to fit to screen to see the whole family at once. The mark system

one component · three sizes · dark green

The mark is a shelf of four spines, one leaning, in a ruled square, stamped in dark green #034706. Every piece uses it, at three fixed sizes and never a fourth. Everything else on the sheet prints black.

Rules
  • Square, ruled at 5.8% of its own width, so the frame scales with the mark.
  • Always #034706 — the same green on press and on screen. There is no print-only variant. Never a third colour. The black drawing is used only when the whole job prints in black alone.
  • Never smaller than 7 mm — the leaning spine closes up below that.
  • Clear space on all sides equal to one spine width.
  • The seal never sits inside a rule, box, or panel other than its own.
B

The family — Foolscap Mk II

six pieces · one mark · black + green

Six pieces, all carrying the same seal at its specified size. The head sheet is a pad — glued at the head, 50 sheets on a chipboard back — and it is not a plain letterhead: it is the accession record. It runs in the order the work is actually done: volume (the book as it is in hand, with the issue points ticked), accession (the transaction), condition on the antiquarian trade grades, then notes. Fields are set on a twelve-column grid and sized to their content, so the length of the rule tells you what is expected on it — a date is eight characters wide, a title runs the full measure. What the design takes from Japanese stationery is restraint and a stamp: the boxed seal, the stacked wordmark, a 34 mm hairline in place of the full-width rule, confined ruling instead of ruling that bleeds across the page, and the form reference set vertically in the left margin.

One bookmark, not five. The collection is ticked by hand rather than printed, so the bookmark is one item printed one way, and adding a collection to the library never makes the stock in the drawer obsolete. It also puts the bookmark in the same idiom as everything else in the family: a form you fill in.

Item 06 — bookmark
  • Every copy is identical. The collection is written on by hand, not printed, so there is one version of this item and reprints run from the same artwork file. A new collection is entered on the Other rule.
  • Imposition: 5 up on A4 (210 × 297 mm), or 10 up on SRA3 (320 × 450 mm).
  • Cutting: straight guillotine cuts on all four edges. Nothing on this item is die-cut.
  • Grain direction: the grain must run parallel to the 210 mm dimension, so the bookmark lies flat between the pages of a book rather than curling.
  • Stock, colours, bleed, proofing and delivery for this item are as set out in section C and are not restated here.

Notes to the printer — whole job
  • Quantities are given on the order. Please quote each of the six items separately at 250, 500 and 1,000.
  • Colours: two spot colours across the whole job — black, and one green. There is no four-colour process on any item. The green is drawn at #034706, which is the same value the mark carries on screen. No Pantone number is specified: please match to a drawdown on the running stock and send it for approval before the run. Allow for the ink drying back and losing density on uncoated stock when choosing the match, but do not alter the supplied artwork to compensate.
  • Artwork supplied as PDF/X-4, one file per item, at 100% size. Two spot channels only, named Black and HSL Green; no CMYK or RGB objects anywhere in the files. Fonts embedded. No bleed allowance in the files. Trim marks offset 5 mm.
  • No bleed anywhere. All artwork is inset at least 6 mm from the trim on every item, so a 3 mm trim allowance is sufficient throughout.
  • No coatings, no varnish, no lamination, no foil, no embossing. The uncoated surface has to stay writable — these are forms that get filled in with pen and pencil.
  • No die-cutting anywhere except the labels (item 05), which are cut through the face material only. Every other item is cut on the guillotine.
  • Grain long on all items, running parallel to the longer dimension. This matters most on the pad: across the grain, the sheets ripple at the glued head.
  • Pad build (01): 50 sheets, flexible padding compound at the head, 1.5 mm chipboard back, no tape strip. Knock the stack up square to the head edge before gluing.
  • Envelopes (03): keep all printing 5 mm clear of the gummed areas and of the flap fold. Print the reverse after the face, allowing for the extra thickness at the flap when setting up.
  • Registration: the green never overprints or butts the black — the two colours meet only as separate elements on the same sheet, so ±0.15 mm is ample. Do not add trapping, spread or choke to the supplied artwork.
  • If the job is quoted as one colour: the seal prints black and nothing else on any item changes. Do not substitute a tint or screen of black for the green.
  • Proofs: one wet proof on the running stock for the pad (01) and the bookmark (06), for approval before the run. A laser proof is sufficient for the other four items.
  • Delivery to 421 Hudson St., New York, NY 10014. Labels on sheets, not rolls; bookmarks banded in 50s; pads shrink-wrapped in 5s.
