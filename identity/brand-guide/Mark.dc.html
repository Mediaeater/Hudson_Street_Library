01 · The Mark
Four spines, one leaning.

The whole drawing is four rectangles on a 512 square, the last one turned nine degrees. It began as the favicon and now stands at the head of every page, stamped in the green. On stationery it sits inside a hairline box and becomes the seal.

9°

The fourth spine rotates nine degrees about its foot, so the shelf reads as in use, not abandoned. Nothing else moves. Do not redraw, thin, thicken, or straighten the spines, and do not add a fifth.

The source of truth is the inline SVG in the site header and the colophon. Reproduce those four rectangles exactly.

Clear space and minimum size

Clear space is one spine width on all sides. A spine is one tenth of the mark's height. In print the mark is never set below seven millimetres, the size at which the leaning spine closes up and the shelf becomes a smudge; on stationery it runs at 15, 10, or 7 mm only. On screen the floor is 16 pixels, the favicon.

15 · 10 · 7 mm

One hard rule. Always inline the SVG with an explicit fill. The favicon file self-inverts in OS dark mode and vanishes as an image on light backgrounds. The favicon is the one place that inversion is wanted.
