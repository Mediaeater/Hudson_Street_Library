The paler greens are the same green thinned, not different colours. The ramp holds one hue across every step; the version before it wandered from yellow-green at the light end to a colder green at the dark end and read as grey in the middle. 600 clears 6.96 : 1 on white; 400 and 500 are for fills and tints, never for text on white.

In print the green is a spot ink, matched to a drawdown on the running stock, never built from process colours. No Pantone number is specified; the chip is the standard.

Success, warning, and error are interface status colours. They are never the brand and never leave the interface.
