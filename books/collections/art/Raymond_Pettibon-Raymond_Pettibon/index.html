About This Book

This comprehensive monograph presents the work of Raymond Pettibon, one of America's most influential contemporary artists known for his distinctive black-and-white drawings that combine text and image. Pettibon's work emerged from the punk rock scene of 1980s Los Angeles, where he created album covers and flyers for bands like Black Flag.

The book showcases Pettibon's unique artistic vision that blends high and low culture, literary references, and social commentary. His drawings often feature surfers, baseball players, pop culture icons, and political figures, accompanied by fragments of text that create narrative tension and cultural critique.

Published as part of Rizzoli's prestigious Classics series, this volume includes essays by leading art critics and curators, providing context for Pettibon's significant contribution to contemporary American art. The artist's official website at raypettibon.com offers additional resources and current work.
