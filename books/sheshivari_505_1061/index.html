505

by Izet Sheshivari

Description

A compilation bringing together reprints of 28 out-of-print artist's notepads originally published by Sheshivari in his own publishing house, Boabooks, between 2008 and 2011. These original notepads featured contributions (images and text) from various contemporary artists, including Olivier Mosset, Isabelle Cornaro, and Jérémie Gindre, among others. The book is primarily an art publication and a documentation of Sheshivari's curatorial and publishing work with Boabooks, allowing readers to view the development of the series as a whole.
