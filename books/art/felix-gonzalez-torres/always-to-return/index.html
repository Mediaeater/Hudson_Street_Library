About This Book

This monograph examines the artist's relationship to portraiture, expanding the genre from static representations of individuals to one with the capacity to change, remain resonant, and encourage collaboration.

The publication accompanies a major exhibition at the Smithsonian and combines exhibition documentation with new scholarship and archival materials. Co-published with the National Portrait Gallery and the Archives of American Art, Smithsonian Institution.
