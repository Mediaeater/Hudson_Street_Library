AKINA Publishers

by Various-Artists

Description

About the project: 1 month of shooting, 1 year in the making, 3AM is an experimental collective project unlike any others, a psychedelic experience brought to you by the long-term collaboration between AKINA and AM Projects. AM primarily means ‘After Midnight’, but it can also refer to ‘a.m’ (ante meridiem or before midday). AM projects is set up by independent photographers as a space to manifest themselves internationally as a likeminded group via jointly curated exhibitions and publications.
