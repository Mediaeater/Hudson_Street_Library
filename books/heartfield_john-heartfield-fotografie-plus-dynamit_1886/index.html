John Heartfield: Fotografie plus Dynamit

by John Heartfield
Hirmer Verlag · 2020 · Hardcover

John Heartfield: Fotografie plus Dynamit is the catalog for a major Akademie der Künste retrospective on John Heartfield (1891-1968), the Berlin Dada artist who turned photomontage into a weapon against fascism and war.

Co-published by the Akademie der Künste and Hirmer Verlag and edited by Angela Lammert, Anna Schultz, and Rosa von der Schulenburg, the 311-page volume draws on the Academy's own Heartfield Archive to survey his career across 250 color reproductions, from his Berlin Dada beginnings through his AIZ covers attacking Hitler and the Nazi regime to his postwar design work in East Germany. The exhibition opened at the Akademie der Künste in March 2020 before traveling to Museum de Fundatie in Zwolle and the Royal Academy of Arts in London.

Heartfield co-founded Berlin Dada with George Grosz and his brother, the writer Wieland Herzfelde, pairing found photographs with blunt captions to devastating satirical effect. His AIZ covers of the 1930s remain touchstones of anti-fascist visual culture and a foundational reference for later photomontage and collage artists working in political protest.
