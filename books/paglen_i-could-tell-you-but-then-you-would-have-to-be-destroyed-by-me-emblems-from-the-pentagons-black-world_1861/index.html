I Could Tell You But Then You Would Have to Be Destroyed By Me: Emblems from the Pentagon's Black World

by Trevor Paglen
Melville House · 2010 · Paperback

Trevor Paglen's I Could Tell You But Then You Would Have to Be Destroyed by Me collects more than seventy insignia patches worn on classified U.S. military and intelligence programs, reading their cartoonish, occult imagery as an accidental portrait of the government's secret world.

Assembled through Freedom of Information Act requests and tips from current and former operators, the book reproduces each patch in full color with Paglen's decoding of its symbolism: a grey alien captioned "to serve man," six-pointed stars standing in for Area 51, red stars marking Nevada test ranges, and mottoes too obscene to print. This 2010 Melville House paperback updates Paglen's 2008 hardcover with new patches and research.

Paglen (b. 1974), an American artist and geographer trained at UC Berkeley and the School of the Art Institute of Chicago, makes the classified infrastructure of surveillance and military power visible through photography, sculpture, and investigation. This book followed Torture Taxi and preceded Blank Spots on the Map he later filmed for Citizenfour, won a MacArthur Fellowship in 2017, and had the survey Sights Unseen at the Smithsonian American Art Museum in 2018.
