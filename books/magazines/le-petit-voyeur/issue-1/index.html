Le Petit Voyeur Issue 1

2013

About This Issue

The inaugural issue of Le Petit Voyeur, a curated photography magazine showcasing exceptional contemporary photographers and artists. This first volume established the publication's distinctive large format and high production values.

Cover artwork by Aaron Nagel.
