Cy Twombly: Fifty Years of Works on Paper

by Cy Twombly
Schirmer/Mosel · 2004 · Hardcover

A survey of Cy Twombly's drawings, collages, and paintings on paper from 1953 to 2002, issued as the revised and expanded second edition of the catalogue first published as Cy Twombly at the Hermitage.

The State Hermitage Museum in St. Petersburg organised the exhibition in 2003 to mark Twombly's 75th birthday, with Julie Sylvester as curator. Its 84 works came from the artist's studio, and many had not been shown before. This edition was prepared for the showing at the Whitney Museum of American Art, New York, from January 27 to May 8, 2005. It contains an essay by Simon Schama, a new text by Sylvester, Roland Barthes's 1976 essay Non Multa Sed Multum and a foreword by the Whitney's director, Adam D. Weinberg. Biographical notes, a selected exhibition history and a bibliography were added.

Twombly worked on paper with pencil, ballpoint pen, crayon, and wall paint, usually in series and often in collage. The selection runs from early monotypes to the mythological cycles of his later years. The exhibition travelled to Munich, Paris and London before New York, and Twombly drew the cover for this English-language edition.
