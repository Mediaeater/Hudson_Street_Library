Purple Magazine

An influential French fashion and contemporary culture magazine founded in Paris in 1992 by Elein Fleiss and Olivier Zahm. Purple emerged as a reaction against the superficial glamour of the 1980s, pioneering the "anti-fashion" aesthetic and becoming synonymous with the new realism in fashion photography through collaborators like Juergen Teller, Terry Richardson, Wolfgang Tillmans, and Mario Sorrenti.

Surveillance Index

Books about watchers and the watched. The photobooks and artists' books of the art wing sit here with the histories, theory and reportage of the Surveillance & Dissent wing. The index is kept in two editions, and the first was published as a printed guide to one hundred of the books.

Surveillance Index Edition Two

The second edition of the Surveillance Index: every book in the index that is not in Edition One. It holds the later photobooks and artists' books of the art wing and the whole of the Surveillance & Dissent wing.

TISSUE Magazine

Independent art and media magazine published from 2011 to 2022, devoted — in its own words — to life and death. Across five print issues, TISSUE mixed photography, nightlife, sexuality, and contemporary culture with a restless, collaged design language. The library holds the complete print run, N°1 through N°5.

Toilet Paper

An artists' magazine created by Maurizio Cattelan and Pierpaolo Ferrari, featuring provocative photography and surreal imagery. Since 2010, Toilet Paper has combined commercial photography with twisted narrative tableaux, challenging the limits of the contemporary art economy.
