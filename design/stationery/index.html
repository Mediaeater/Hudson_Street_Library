B

Selected — Foolscap, developed

full administrative family · 1 ink + collection accent

The family extends from one sheet. The compliment slip is exactly one third of the letterhead (203 × 110), so it gangs 3-up on the same stock with zero waste and slides flat into the DL envelope. The catalogue card is the true library standard at 125 × 75 mm. Bookmarks stay 55 × 210, 5-up on A4 — the colour hairline at the head is the only plate that changes between collections, so the whole series is one press run plus five ink swaps.

Bookmarks55 × 210 mm300 gsm uncoated board, matte2 — black + accent 5-up on A4, 10-up on SRA3. Straight guillotine cuts, no die, no bleed (art inset 6 mm). Only the head hairline changes per collection.

Why odd sizes stay cheap: the envelope is a stock size bought blank and overprinted; the compliment slip and bookmarks gang to whole numbers on standard parent sheets with straight cuts. One or two spot inks on uncoated throughout — no coatings, no varnish, no die-cutting, nothing needing a bleed. The only true premium is the foolscap trim itself.
