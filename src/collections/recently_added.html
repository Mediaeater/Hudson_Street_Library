N7433.4.K56 A4 2024

Ayoung Kim: Synthetic Storyteller

Ayoung Kim

Imprint: Seoul: The Floorplan, 2024.

Physical Description: 400 pages: illustrations; 11.2 x 18.3 cm

ISBN: 979-11-985617-0-1

Accession Date: November 23, 2025

A selection of artworks produced between 2007 and 2022, featuring still images, installation views, and critical essays. The monograph encompasses Ayoung Kim's multimedia practice including video, moving image, VR, game simulation, sonic fiction, diagrams, and texts that explore geopolitics, mythology, technology, and futuristic themes. Published in Korean and English.

Keywords: Video Art, VR, Game Art, Sonic Fiction, Multimedia, Geopolitics, Mythology, Korean Art

N6537.P745 A4 2025

Richard Prince: Folksongs

Richard Prince

Imprint: Fulton Ryder, 2025.

Physical Description: 193 pages: 193 color illustrations; 29.8 x 22.2 cm

ISBN: 9798985236859

Accession Date: November 7, 2025

An artist's book and monograph showcasing Prince's conceptual work across multiple mediums. Features new body of paintings alongside studio photos and documentation of various sculptures and installations from his Upstate series. Works incorporate collage, impasto, and oil stick techniques with close-up shots of eyeballs, sutured skin and mouths with a cigarette.

NE2012.G69 A4 2025

Goya: The Disasters of War

Francisco de Goya

Imprint: Madrid: La Fábrica, 2025.

Physical Description: 176 pages: 80 etchings; 30 x 25 cm

ISBN: 9788410024632

Accession Date: October 28, 2025

Based on the original 1863 edition, this presentation of Goya's 80 etchings decries the consequences of war through his bleak, gruesome and unflinching imagery from the Peninsular War (1808-14). Faithfully reproduces the first edition published 35 years after Goya's death.

Keywords: War, Etchings, Spanish Art, Historical, Political Art, Printmaking

TR647.W65 A4 2025

Arthur Rimbaud in New York

David Wojnarowicz

Imprint: Milan: Skira, 2025.

Physical Description: 208 pages: 150 color images; 28 x 24 cm

ISBN: 9788857254067

Accession Date: October 28, 2025

Documents Wojnarowicz's 1978-79 photographs of a man wearing a paper mask of Arthur Rimbaud. Essays by Antonio Sergio Bessa, Nicholas Martin, and Craig Dworkin contextualize the work within punk culture, downtown Manhattan, and queer history. Includes interview with photographer Allen Frame.

Keywords: Photography, LGBTQ Art, Performance Art, Punk, Downtown New York

N6537.T453 A4 2025

Stay away from nothing

Paul Thek and Peter Hujar

Imprint: New York: Primary Information, 2025.

Physical Description: 192 pages: illustrations, photographs; 9 x 12 inches

Accession Date: October 24, 2025

Explores the relationship between artists Paul Thek and Peter Hujar through over fifty letters, postcards, drawings, and ephemera documenting their travels and creative work from 1956-1975. Includes Hujar's photographs of Thek from the Palermo catacombs and studio portraits during the creation of "The Tomb."

Keywords: Photography, queer art, American artists, artist correspondence, 1960s-1970s, New York art

PN6728.R49 M37 2025

Rythm Mastr: This Is How It Begins

Kerry James Marshall

Imprint: New York: Rizzoli Electa, 2025.

Physical Description: 1 volume: illustrations (color); 30 cm

Accession Date: October 20, 2025

Marshall's groundbreaking comic series created in the late 1990s as a direct response to the absence of authentic Black characters and authors in mainstream comics. Features Rythm Mastr and his protégé Farell, superheroes whose powers derive from the seven gods of the Yoruba pantheon.

Keywords: Afrofuturism, graphic novels, African American art, contemporary art, superhero comics
