About This Issue

Purple Magazine (originally known as Purple Prose) is a biannual fashion, art, and culture magazine founded in 1992 in Paris. Known for its avant-garde aesthetic and influential perspective on contemporary fashion and art.

The Revolutions Issue — F/W 2023. Cover featuring Milla Jovovich.

Purple has been instrumental in documenting and shaping underground culture, featuring emerging and established artists, photographers, and writers who challenge conventional aesthetics.

About Purple Magazine

Purple Magazine has become one of the most influential fashion and culture publications, known for its raw, unpolished aesthetic and willingness to challenge mainstream fashion media. The magazine features photography, art, and writing from some of the most important contemporary artists and has helped launch numerous careers in fashion photography.
