Le Petit Voyeur Issue 7

2021 • Special Edition

About This Issue

The seventh and final issue of Le Petit Voyeur features 25 artists including acclaimed photographers Viviane Sassen and Martin Parr, the collaborative duo Honey Long & Prue Stent, and legendary artist Futura. An advertising-free independent publication.

Front cover photography by Honey Long & Prue Stent. Back cover painting by Futura. The cover features soft touch lamination with embossed and UV lacquered details.
