About This Booklet

Part of a series of four booklets created for the Georgia O'Keeffe collaboration with Comme des Garçons. This unique partnership brings together the visionary aesthetics of American modernist painter Georgia O'Keeffe with the avant-garde Japanese fashion house founded by Rei Kawakubo.

The booklets showcase O'Keeffe's iconic imagery alongside Comme des Garçons' distinctive design sensibility, creating a dialogue between fine art and fashion that exemplifies both brands' commitment to artistic innovation.
