Folksongs

Richard Prince

About This Book

This artist's book showcases previously unpublished paintings alongside studio photography. It documents the "Upstate" series—photographs Prince captured in upstate New York—plus sculptures and installations.

The work features collage and impasto techniques with gestural marks referencing his earlier pieces, creating a comprehensive view of Prince's artistic practice.
