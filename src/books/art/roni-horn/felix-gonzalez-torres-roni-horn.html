About This Book

This photographic essay explores the artistic dialogue between Felix Gonzalez-Torres and Roni Horn, initiated in 1990 when Gonzalez-Torres encountered Horn's work Gold Field (1980/82).

The publication examines four iconic pieces: Untitled (For Stockholm) (1992) and Untitled (Blood) (1992) by Gonzalez-Torres, alongside Well and Truly (2009-10) and a.k.a. (2008-09) by Horn. Images were captured during a 2022 exhibition at the Bourse de Commerce-Pinault Collection in Paris, revealing both artists' radical visual vocabularies as well as their shared passion for language, writing, and poetry.

The work emphasizes themes of doubling, duality, repetition, and identity, exploring the inexpressible and the immeasurable.
