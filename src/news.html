January 24, 2026

New Artist Books & Signed Acquisitions

Exciting new additions to the collection this week, including two signed Lisa Yuskavage monographs acquired at Strand Books' artist signing event. The collection now includes the 2006 Museo Tamayo exhibition catalog and Phaidon's 2026 Contemporary Artists Series volume, both signed by the artist. These join recent architecture acquisitions from MOS (Michael Meredith and Hilary Sample): Public Spaces NY (Park Books, 2025) and the updated entry for Vacant Spaces NY (Actar, 2021), part of their ongoing documentation of New York City's urban fabric. Both MOS volumes are beautifully designed by Alex Lin / Studio Lin.

Also new: Beni Bischof's Meta Cars (Nieves, 2025), transforming found car images into digital distortions; Lawrence Weiner's Revue Faire #49 (Éditions Empire, 2024), exploring his relationship with typography; and Maxime Muller's Safe Word (Les Éditions Rien Ne Va Plus, 2023), a 300-page risograph publication from his photographic archives.

November 2025

Winter–Spring 2025 Internship Opportunity

Hudson Street Library is seeking a Winter–Spring 2025 intern with a strong interest in photobooks, visual culture, and archival work. This internship offers hands-on experience supporting the organization and preservation of our growing photobook collection. The intern will assist with cataloging, condition checks, metadata entry, and developing archival systems that improve long-term accessibility. Ideal candidates are detail-oriented, curious about art and photography, and eager to learn the fundamentals of library and archive practice. This is a great opportunity for students exploring library science, art history, or cultural heritage fields.

To apply, send your resume and a brief statement of interest to info@hudsonstreetlibrary.com

New Magazines Collections Page

We've launched a dedicated magazines page showcasing our growing collection of design, photography, and culture magazines. The collection includes complete or near-complete runs of Apartamento (34 issues), Toilet Paper by Cattelan & Ferrari (20 issues), Purple Books (27 issues), Erik Kessels' Useful Photography series (15 issues), and more. Each magazine series now has its own dedicated page with covers and details for individual issues.

November 24, 2025

New Photography Acquisition: Les Oiseaux

We're excited to announce the addition of Les Oiseaux by Lola Raban-Oliva and Jean-René Etienne to our collection. This unique photobook documents the Notre-Dame Cathedral fire from an unprecedented aerial perspective, presenting 97 screenshots captured from the artists' window two kilometers from the blaze.

Enhanced Tag System & Search

We've significantly improved our book tagging system, increasing coverage from 18.5% to 39.4% of the collection. Books are now enriched with keywords inferred from titles, authors, and publishers, making it easier to discover related materials through our search interface.
